∼0.3 WhGoogle Search: Google’s own 2009 disclosure, the only figure it has published
∼0.3–3+ WhAI query: OpenAI’s 2025 figure, up to independent high-complexity estimates

The widely repeated “10× more energy” claim traces to a 2023 estimate built on older hardware and longer assumed responses. More recent company-disclosed figures put a typical AI query much closer to a search — though neither company publishes full methodology, so real uncertainty remains. What’s not in dispute: a search is one index lookup; an AI query is a fresh neural network computation, repeated per token — a fundamentally heavier piece of infrastructure to run, regardless of the exact multiplier.
