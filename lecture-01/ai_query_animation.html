Step 5 of 5 · Core AI Workload Distinctions
Training vs. Inference
Model Training
Offline · Months of Compute
  • • Goal: Learn parameters & weights from huge datasets.
  • • Compute: Tens of thousands of GPUs tied together for months.
  • • Math: Forward pass + Backpropagation (updating weights).
Model Inference
Real-Time · Your Query Right Now
  • • Goal: Generate a response for a specific prompt.
  • • Compute: Seconds or milliseconds on pooled data center GPUs.
  • • Math: Forward pass only (weights are fixed/frozen).
